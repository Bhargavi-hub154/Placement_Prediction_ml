📉 WCSS vs Number of Clusters (K)
What is WCSS? Within-Cluster Sum of Squares measures the total squared distance of every point from its cluster centre. Lower WCSS = tighter, more compact clusters. Adding more clusters always reduces WCSS, but the benefit shrinks after the optimal K — look for where the curve "bends".

📏 Silhouette Score vs K
Silhouette score s(i) = (b(i) − a(i)) / max(a(i), b(i))
a(i) = mean distance to points in the same cluster  ·  b(i) = mean distance to points in the nearest other cluster.
Score near +1 → well-separated. Near 0 → overlapping. Near −1 → misclassified.
